Subject: Agent incidents reach regulators in the US, India and Australia

Here is what happened in the agentic state around the world over the past week.

• Investigations and rules after agent incidents (United States, India, Australia) — The FTC sent [civil subpoenas to OpenAI and Anthropic](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=dbd1f880f9d4). It asks about agents that hacked websites and submitted 19 visa applications without clear instruction. India's CERT-In issued an [advisory on agents as attack vectors](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3d373545471c) and now [reviews mandatory incident disclosure](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3fd9029d78a5). An Australian parliamentary committee proposed a [duty of care for agent developers](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=77e0e0b97d7c). In the US, CISA and NSA also published [guidance to secure agent test sandboxes](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=566129759a77).
• Agents in cyber attack and defence (United States, Japan) — Army Cyber Command started [Task Force Lexington](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=04b430eb2e05), which gives cyber protection team roles to agents. The Department of Transportation builds [cyber defence](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=c2adaca8ee1d) that quarantines risky actions without human approval. Japan's government [investigates a wave of attacks](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=36fde42e1ac6) that used an autonomous program to find weaknesses.
• The Hong Kong Monetary Authority published its [ART framework](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=ff1c85a8c7c6) for AI in banking. It proposes a registry of material AI agents.
• Finland's Ministry of Finance launched [Agentic Finland](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=2512e96faab5), a strategy for agents across central government, wellbeing services counties and municipalities.
• Civil servants build their own agents (Japan, South Korea) — Japan's Digital Agency gives [two agent tools](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=6cde39e24f54) to about 180,000 staff, with tiered permissions. South Korea's interior ministry runs a [challenge](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=ded7864f0321) to turn staff-built tools into shared services on sovereign models.
• Italy's labour ministry runs [AppLI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=01b1bed156b3), a national assistant for job search and training. It had over 60,000 users in its first year. Its agent design does not depend on one language model.
• Tay Ninh province in Vietnam plans an [AI-native government](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=7e88fa1d0ea0) by 2030. Agents will plan and do administrative tasks for civil servants.

Two weeks ago, the story was agents that broke rules. Last week, the US, the UK and the EU answered with rules. This week, the response spreads to India and Australia, and it moves from rules to enforcement. Seven records deal with agent incidents, investigations or containment.

Agents also change cyber security. Governments use them to hunt threats, and attackers use them to find weaknesses. Cyber security appears in 23% of the week's records, up from 17% in the previous eight weeks. Governance appears in 59% of records, up from 38%.

Many records now set levels of autonomy instead of a single yes or no. Japan gives staff tiered agent permissions. Utah [approved a pilot](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9a230e991373) where an AI prescribes acne treatment, with physician checks that decrease in stages. Colorado's draft [ADMT rules](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=7b0527a804da) require human review with override power. The mean autonomy level rose to 3.3, from 3.1.
