Subject: Governments set rules for agents that buy and pay

Here is what the agentic state did around the world last week.

• Rules for agents that buy and pay (European Union, Taiwan, Singapore) — The European Commission proposed [rules for AI purchasing assistants](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3a2f6bf8fd7f) in the Digital Fairness Act. Users would set the agent's autonomy level at first use and get confirmations and withdrawal rights. Taiwan's digital ministry will [study a risk framework for agent shopping](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=d3d531c23cce), with liability, authorisation scope and identity checks. The Monetary Authority of Singapore announced an [Agentic Money Sandbox](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=8664d0664f64) for payments that agents start. A national finance mission will also [trial agents that pay when set conditions are met](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=86d7b4bde73b).
• US states take OpenAI to court over agent incidents (United States) — California's Department of Justice [sued OpenAI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9e762afa6190) over more than 80 incidents where the company lost control of its agents. Florida's Attorney General [asked a court to stop OpenAI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3b4ebfe9edcd) from developing new models without outside oversight.
• Agents in cyber attack and defence (United States, Germany) — The FBI [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=2451a33382d9) that attackers now use ARTEX, an open-source penetration-testing agent, to run intrusions at machine speed. A German [draft intelligence law](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=633aa70e4dbb) would let AI systems start cyber countermeasures without prior human approval.
• Brazil's Ministry of Management and Innovation announced [transactional AI for Contrata+Brasil](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=d97b2375012a). Microentrepreneurs and family farmers who sell to the government can book services and register in databases through a conversation. The tool completes the operation, not only answers.
• Vietnam's tax authority [scaled an AI call centre and chatbot](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=bb45d775820d) in eTax Mobile. The call centre phones taxpayers about debts and travel restrictions. The system serves over 3 million business households.
• South Korea's health ministry pilots a [public healthcare AI network](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=72dc0432ddb3). Three agents manage transfers, surgery, discharge and referrals between regional public hospitals. A referral document now takes one minute to draft, not ten.
• National plans for agents (Cuba, Morocco) — Cuba's Ministry of Communications issued [Resolution 24/2026](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=73194f5575a6), a binding plan to bring AI into all state bodies by 2030. It moves step by step from chatbots to autonomous execution. It is Cuba's first record in the Observatory. Morocco's digital ministry builds [AI agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b89aa4f3f4cd) for business compliance and for drafting bills and decrees.

This week, agents move from the public office into the market. Five records in four jurisdictions deal with agents that buy, pay or move money for people. In the US, Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=413c3cd50266) that banks cannot yet check that agents follow customer instructions. The previous eight weeks had only a few such records, mostly from one retailer.

The questions are the same in each place. Who gave the agent permission? How far may it go? Who pays when it goes too far? The EU proposal leaves that last question to national contract law. India's IT ministry prepares a [consultation paper](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=fda8a3932723) that may define agents by their authority to act.

Liability also moves into courts. Last week, Australia and the UK took standing powers after agent incidents. This week, two US states use consumer-protection law against an AI lab. Both cases cite agents that reached Australian government systems. At the same time, the FBI and the German bill show machines that attack and machines that answer, with no human in between. Compliance and supervision records rose to 17% of the week, from 12%.
