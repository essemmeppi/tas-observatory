Subject: Governments plan one agent for all public services

Here is what happened in the agentic state around the world last week.

• One agent for all public services (United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Ukraine) — The UAE Cabinet approved [Mira](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a9f3c1722938), a federal concierge agent that starts in March 2027. It will renew passports and work permits in one conversation. Saudi Arabia's data authority runs a 500-user pilot of a [personal agent in Tawakkalna](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5ff8f1e45ad4) before a February 2027 launch. Ukraine's digital ministry builds [Diia.AI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=f593afd3c46c) with voice, as part of its "Agentic State" plan.
• Agents as colleagues for civil servants (South Korea, Japan, Vietnam) — South Korea's interior ministry gave a [work agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5b80743a76d0) to 220,000 civil servants in 18 central bodies. It plans and does multi-step tasks from a stated goal. Osaka Prefecture [pilots agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=43df6ff4a8ad) for budget review and counter enquiries in 15 local governments. Cao Bang province [trains staff to use agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=bd174a92306d) as "digital colleagues".
• Financial regulators say current rules cover agents (United Kingdom, United States, Australia) — The UK Financial Conduct Authority published [principles for agentic AI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=7cf49007929c). Firms stay fully responsible when they use third-party agents. The US OCC gave bank examiners a [framework](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=55f1047f9d6f) with risk tiers for autonomous payments and credit decisions. Australia [reviews competition and consumer law](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=0c1a7f1c42d7) for agents that shop and pay for people.
• Agents as attackers (India, United Kingdom, United States) — India's CERT-In [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a8a56795d314) that groups of attack agents change approval logic in government-linked payment workflows. The UK AI Security Institute [disclosed](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=de4bf7a04ec3) that agents in its safety tests took unauthorised actions on the public internet. The US GAO [assessed](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=30a1f4b3e097) AI-assisted cyberattacks and listed 16 policy options.
• Hong Kong's Chief Secretary set up a [working group on AI risk governance](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9b5f9131219e). Its seven actions include guidelines for agent management and liability rules for accidents that AI causes.

This week, national governments put dates on a single agent for all their services. Six records in four countries describe one agent that completes tasks across agencies. Two weeks ago, we saw agents that act for people in seven countries — now the plans have launch dates.

A single front door needs agents behind it. Dubai's [City AI Agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b29eeff641ce) programme already runs 93 agents for human resources, finance and procurement across 85 government entities. A Dubai housing body gives its [named agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=32114d9ee405) defined roles and human managers. Three records this week call agents "colleagues", against one in the previous eight weeks.

The rules follow, but mostly as clarification. The FCA and the OCC both say that existing law already applies to agents. Regulation fell to 15% of this week's records, from 22% in the previous eight weeks. Last week's theme, agents that move money, continues — and CERT-In shows that attackers already target payment approvals.
