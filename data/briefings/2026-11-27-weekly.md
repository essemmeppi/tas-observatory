Subject: The Agentic State: One agent for every public service

Hi @name,

This week the Observatory collected 59 new initiatives from 20 countries. Here are the most interesting ones:

- **United Arab Emirates** — The Cabinet approved [Mira](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a9f3c1722938), a concierge agent for all federal services from March 2027. In one conversation it renews passports, Emirates IDs and work permits, and fills in the forms from state records.
- **Saudi Arabia** — 500 people now test a [personal agent in Tawakkalna](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5ff8f1e45ad4), an app with over 36 million users. You ask in your own dialect, and the agent follows the request across government bodies until it is done. Launch is in February 2027.
- **South Korea** — The interior ministry gave a [work agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5b80743a76d0) to 220,000 civil servants in 18 central bodies. You give it a goal, and it plans and does the steps.
- **India** — CERT-In [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a8a56795d314) that groups of attack agents target government-linked payment workflows. They change the approval logic, so unauthorised payments go through legitimate channels.
- **Japan** — The Agency for Cultural Affairs uses an [agent that finds pirated manga and anime](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=570483e3a4a5) on websites and social media. In its first year it flagged 1,168 posts, and 84% were illegal.
- **Oman** — In its first appearance on the Observatory, the transport and IT ministry's ["Engineer It with AI" competition](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=bcca6a19639c) produced 15 agentic applications for government, from more than 300 teams.

Two Gulf states now have dates for something we have watched for months: one agent for every public service. You talk to it, and it deals with the ministries for you. Ukraine builds the same thing with [Diia.AI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=f593afd3c46c) – in wartime.

The interesting part is what sits behind that front door. Dubai already runs [93 agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b29eeff641ce) for HR, finance and procurement across 85 government entities, and Korea gives agents to its civil servants. A single agent for citizens only works if the back office runs on agents too.

Meanwhile, most regulators say the old rules still apply. This week the UK [FCA](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=7cf49007929c) and the US [OCC](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=55f1047f9d6f) both said that firms stay responsible for what their agents do. Hong Kong went one step further: its new [working group](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9b5f9131219e) will write liability rules for accidents that AI causes.
