Here is our monthly digest from the Observatory. In November we collected 195 initiatives from 41 countries, 7 of them for the first time. Here are the most important ones.

- One agent for every public service — The **UAE** Cabinet approved [Mira](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a9f3c1722938), a federal concierge agent from March 2027. **Saudi Arabia** pilots a [personal agent in Tawakkalna](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5ff8f1e45ad4) before a February 2027 launch. In the **US**, the DOGE Service plans [agents on America.gov](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=dec8a5918175) that complete tasks such as tax refunds.
- National plans for an agentic state — **Portugal**'s Minister of State Reform presented an ["agentic state"](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3203e117506d) at Web Summit. **Germany**'s digital ministry pilots an [Agentic Agency Framework](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=64ee9b3104c6), and some pilots cut processing time by over 90%. **Ukraine** builds [Diia.AI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=f593afd3c46c) as part of its own agentic state plan. **Cuba** issued [Resolution 24/2026](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=73194f5575a6), which takes all state bodies from chatbots to autonomous execution by 2030.
- Agents for civil servants — **South Korea** gave a [work agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5b80743a76d0) to 220,000 civil servants. **Japan**'s Digital Agency opened [two agent environments](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3e58be072c3d) for about 180,000 staff. **Dubai** runs [93 agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b29eeff641ce) for HR, finance and procurement across 85 government entities.
- Services that act before you ask — **South Korea** will pilot [automated benefit enrolment](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=4c4f1090b9b8) in 19 services, so eligible people do not have to apply. **Poland** builds a [voice agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=453ff96f1a80) that books medical appointments by landline. In **Italy**, Milan and INPS issue the [disability pass](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=0860df0dbf0e) in 13 hours instead of three weeks.
- The deployer is responsible — Four **UK** regulators wrote in a [joint letter](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=e1f393fb9c57) that autonomy does not reduce liability. The **US** OCC gave bank examiners an [agent risk framework](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=55f1047f9d6f). **Hong Kong** set up a [working group](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9b5f9131219e) that will write liability rules for accidents that AI causes.
- Rules for agents that buy and pay — The **EU** proposed [rules for AI purchasing assistants](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3a2f6bf8fd7f). **Singapore** opened an [Agentic Money Sandbox](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=8664d0664f64). **Taiwan** will [study agent shopping](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=d3d531c23cce), and **Australia** [reviews its consumer law](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=0c1a7f1c42d7) for it.
- Incidents go to court — **California** [sued OpenAI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9e762afa6190) over more than 80 incidents where agents escaped test environments. A Pentagon contractor's [coding agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a441ec7f0e70) leaked login data for two months. **Australia** will create an [AI safety regulator](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=f63cdbdee494) after an agent reached non-public Medicare statistics.
- Agents as attackers — **India**'s CERT-In [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a8a56795d314) that attack agents change approval logic in government payment workflows. The **FBI** [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=2451a33382d9) that criminals use ARTEX, an open-source testing agent, for intrusions. A **German** [draft law](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=633aa70e4dbb) would let AI start cyber countermeasures without prior human approval.
- Agents that read the rules — **Argentina** uses [three agents on a 1968 food decree](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=70380ac447b6), and humans check their drafts. **Morocco** builds [agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b89aa4f3f4cd) that help draft bills and decrees.

In October, governments talked about the agentic state. In November, they gave it dates. The UAE and Saudi Arabia will launch a single agent for all public services in early 2027, and Portugal, Germany, Ukraine and Cuba wrote the idea into national plans. Eleven records this month use words like "agentic state" or "agentic government" – in the three months before, there were three.

The design is almost always the same: one agent in front, many agents behind. The front door gets the headlines. But the real work happens in the back office, where Seoul, Tokyo and Dubai now give agents to hundreds of thousands of civil servants.

Last month we asked who is responsible when an agent acts. In November, regulators started to answer: the organisation that deploys it. Mostly they get there with old law – regulation stayed at 17% of records. Where new rules do appear, they follow the money: purchases, payments, bank supervision. And money is where attackers go too, as the CERT-In warning shows.
