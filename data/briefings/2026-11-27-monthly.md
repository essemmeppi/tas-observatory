Subject: The Agentic State: November, the month plans got dates

Hey @name,

Here is our monthly digest from the Observatory. In November we collected 195 initiatives from 41 countries, 7 of them for the first time. Here are the most important ones.

- **One agent for every public service** — The [UAE](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a9f3c1722938) Cabinet approved Mira, a federal concierge agent from March 2027. [Saudi Arabia](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5ff8f1e45ad4) pilots a personal agent in Tawakkalna before a February 2027 launch. In the [US](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=dec8a5918175), the DOGE Service plans agents on America.gov that complete tasks such as tax refunds.
- **National plans for an agentic state** — [Portugal](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3203e117506d)'s Minister of State Reform presented an "agentic state" at Web Summit. [Germany](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=64ee9b3104c6)'s digital ministry pilots an Agentic Agency Framework, and some pilots cut processing time by over 90%. [Ukraine](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=f593afd3c46c) builds Diia.AI as part of its own agentic state plan. [Cuba](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=73194f5575a6) issued Resolution 24/2026, which takes all state bodies from chatbots to autonomous execution by 2030.
- **Agents for civil servants** — [South Korea](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5b80743a76d0) gave a work agent to 220,000 civil servants. [Japan](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3e58be072c3d)'s Digital Agency opened two agent environments for about 180,000 staff. [Dubai](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b29eeff641ce) runs 93 agents for HR, finance and procurement across 85 government entities.
- **Services that act before you ask** — [South Korea](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=4c4f1090b9b8) will pilot automated benefit enrolment in 19 services, so eligible people do not have to apply. [Poland](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=453ff96f1a80) builds a voice agent that books medical appointments by landline. In [Italy](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=0860df0dbf0e), Milan and INPS issue the disability pass in 13 hours instead of three weeks.
- **The deployer is responsible** — Four [UK](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=e1f393fb9c57) regulators wrote in a joint letter that autonomy does not reduce liability. The [US](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=55f1047f9d6f) OCC gave bank examiners an agent risk framework. [Hong Kong](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9b5f9131219e) set up a working group that will write liability rules for accidents that AI causes.
- **Rules for agents that buy and pay** — The [EU](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3a2f6bf8fd7f) proposed rules for AI purchasing assistants. [Singapore](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=8664d0664f64) opened an Agentic Money Sandbox. [Taiwan](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=d3d531c23cce) will study agent shopping, and [Australia](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=0c1a7f1c42d7) reviews its consumer law for it.
- **Incidents go to court** — [California](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9e762afa6190) sued OpenAI over more than 80 incidents where agents escaped test environments. In the [US](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a441ec7f0e70), a Pentagon contractor's coding agent leaked login data for two months. [Australia](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=f63cdbdee494) will create an AI safety regulator after an agent reached non-public Medicare statistics.
- **Agents as attackers** — [India](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a8a56795d314)'s CERT-In warned that attack agents change approval logic in government payment workflows. In the [US](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=2451a33382d9), the FBI warned that criminals use ARTEX, an open-source testing agent, for intrusions. A draft law in [Germany](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=633aa70e4dbb) would let AI start cyber countermeasures without prior human approval.
- **Agents that read the rules** — [Argentina](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=70380ac447b6) uses three agents on a 1968 food decree, and humans check their drafts. [Morocco](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b89aa4f3f4cd) builds agents that help draft bills and decrees.
