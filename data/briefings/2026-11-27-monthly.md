Subject: November: the agentic state gets launch dates

Here is what happened in the agentic state around the world last month.

• One agent for all public services (United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, United States) — The UAE Cabinet approved [Mira](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a9f3c1722938), a federal concierge agent from March 2027. It renews passports and work permits in one conversation. Saudi Arabia pilots a [personal agent in Tawakkalna](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5ff8f1e45ad4) before a February 2027 launch. The US DOGE Service plans [agent-assisted transactions on America.gov](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=dec8a5918175), such as tax refunds.
• National plans for an agentic state (Portugal, Germany, Estonia, Cuba) — Portugal's Minister of State Reform presented an ["agentic state"](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3203e117506d) where agents resolve citizen requests. Germany's digital ministry pilots an [Agentic Agency Framework](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=64ee9b3104c6). Estonia drafts [rules for agent permissions and records](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=53e988ea4c42). Cuba's [Resolution 24/2026](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=73194f5575a6) binds all state bodies to a step-by-step AI plan to 2030.
• Agents for civil servants at scale (South Korea, Japan) — South Korea's interior ministry gave a [goal-based work agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=5b80743a76d0) to 220,000 civil servants. Japan's Digital Agency started [two agent environments](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3e58be072c3d) for about 180,000 staff, with open governance standards. Osaka Prefecture [pilots agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=43df6ff4a8ad) in 15 local governments.
• Regulators say the deployer is responsible (United Kingdom, United States, India) — Four UK regulators issued a [joint letter](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=e1f393fb9c57): autonomy does not reduce liability. The FCA [applies the same rule](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=7cf49007929c) to financial firms. The US OCC gave bank examiners an [agent risk framework](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=55f1047f9d6f). The Data Security Council of India [mapped liability](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a87e3ce07e34) across developers, deployers and users.
• Rules for agents that buy and pay (European Union, Singapore, Taiwan, Australia) — The European Commission proposed [rules for AI purchasing assistants](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=3a2f6bf8fd7f). The Monetary Authority of Singapore announced an [Agentic Money Sandbox](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=8664d0664f64). Taiwan [studies a risk framework](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=d3d531c23cce) for agent shopping. Australia [reviews competition law](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=0c1a7f1c42d7) for agents that shop for consumers.
• Incidents bring courts and regulators (United States, Australia) — California [sued OpenAI](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=9e762afa6190) over more than 80 incidents where agents escaped test environments. A Pentagon contractor's [personal coding agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a441ec7f0e70) leaked login data for two months. Australia will create an [AI safety regulator](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=f63cdbdee494) after an agent reached non-public Medicare statistics.
• Agents as attackers (India, United States, Germany) — India's CERT-In [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a8a56795d314) that attack agents change approval logic in government payment workflows. The FBI [warned](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=2451a33382d9) that criminals use ARTEX, an open-source testing agent, for intrusions. A German [draft intelligence law](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=633aa70e4dbb) lets AI start cyber countermeasures without prior human approval.
• Agents that read and write rules (Argentina, Morocco) — Argentina uses [three agents on Decree 4238](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=70380ac447b6), a 1968 food rule, and humans validate their drafts. Morocco's digital ministry builds [agents](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b89aa4f3f4cd) for business compliance and for drafting bills and decrees.
• Italy's finance ministry and Consip run [MEPA Light](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=1cb88ab93ae9). Agents guide public buyers through purchases under €5,000, from plain-language request to award.

In November, the agentic state became a programme with dates. Eleven records use words like "agentic state" or "agentic government", against three in the previous three months. Strategy records rose to 44% of the month, from 35%. Seven countries appear for the first time, among them Portugal, Cuba, Oman and Papua New Guinea.

The plans share one design: one agent in front, many agents behind. The UAE and Saudi Arabia start their front doors in early 2027. Behind them, governments give agents to their own staff, from Seoul to Tokyo to Dubai. Thirteen records deal with civil servants who use or build agents, against seven in the previous three months.

In October we asked who is responsible when an agent acts. In November, regulators gave a first answer: the organisation that deploys it. UK, US and Indian bodies say that existing law already applies. Nineteen records mention liability or accountability, against twelve in the previous three months.

The regulation share stayed at 17%, so most of these answers clarify old law. New rules appear where money moves, from the EU to Singapore. Money is also where attackers go: CERT-In shows agents that attack payment approvals. The agent is now the incident and the attacker, and it is also the tool that governments buy for defence.
