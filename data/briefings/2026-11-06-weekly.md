Subject: Agents that read the rulebook, and a Pentagon warning

Here is what happened in the agentic state around the world in the past seven days.

• Agents that read the rules (Argentina, United Arab Emirates, South Korea) — Argentina uses [three agents on Decree 4238](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=70380ac447b6), a 1968 food rule. They draft simplifications for humans to validate. Abu Dhabi's food safety authority pilots [machine-readable regulation](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=28abbd2d52e0) for import checks. Two Korean ministries build a [multi-agent permit check](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=921a9c9c0005) for land development.
• A US Department of Defense contractor gave a [personal AI coding agent](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=a441ec7f0e70) administrator access to a sensitive network. It leaked login data for two months. NIST, NSA and CISA published [incident guidance](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=ae717beee45f) for cases where the agent itself causes harm.
• Governments pay for their own AI stack (Canada, India, South Korea) — Canada took an [equity stake in Cohere](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=fd0ac74610b9). India's MeitY launched [Eka-1 and Dhīr](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=c450d5a049f3), domestic models for agentic use that run offline. South Korea's 2027 budget sets aside 4 trillion won for an [autonomous AI model](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=dae635557d34).
• Germany's [KI-Fahrplan 2030](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=b5825dbfb51a) wants agents to run administrative processes end to end where the law permits. The state will buy from European providers as anchor customer.
• Italy's finance ministry and Consip run [MEPA Light](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=1cb88ab93ae9). Agents guide public buyers through purchases under €5,000, from plain-language request to award.
• The UK housing ministry expands [PlanAssist](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=01d8ee4119ac) from 3 to 15 councils. It checks householder planning cases against policy and cut officer time by 58% in early tests.
• Can Tho city in Vietnam put [AI kiosks and assistants](https://observatory.agenticstate.org/#r=ec53ac899375) in service centres. They verify identity and complete requests, over 2,200 in one district since August.

This week, agents take on a new task: they read the law itself. Four records use agents to analyse rules or check cases against them. Before, an agent usually applied a rule that a human had already interpreted. Now the agent reads first, and the human checks.

The Pentagon case shows the limits of that trust. The cause was not a laboratory test, but one worker with an unapproved tool. After a quieter week, five items deal with agent incidents or the response to them.

Both themes help explain a third: governments want to own the stack under their agents. Tech-stack records rose to 36% of the week, from 21% in the previous eight weeks. Abu Dhabi runs its rulebook pilot on local GPUs. A state that lets agents read its laws wants control of the model underneath.
